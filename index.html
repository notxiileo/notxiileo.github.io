Hey, I'm xiileo

Started making Gorilla Tag maps to actually finish something in Blender — a few hundred downloads later, it's the whole job. Block out, model, texture, then make sure it survives a sweaty 8-person lobby. VR keeps me honest: if it doesn't feel good in-headset, you know in four seconds.
